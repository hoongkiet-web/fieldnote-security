Know where you stand before a regulator does.

Expert-led review, not an automated scan. GDPR compliance comes down to judgment calls a script can't make - lawful basis, data processing agreements, breach procedures - so every review here is done by hand, by me.

What it covers

Three phases, all done by hand: a technical review, a structured questionnaire, and a personal write-up of both - no automated scoring at any stage.

PHASE 01

Technical Review

Cookie consent banner check, tracking script detection, privacy policy presence, and HTTPS/data-in-transit check - checked by hand, not run through an automated scanner, so a technically-present-but-non-compliant banner doesn't get waved through.

PHASE 02

Data Practices Questionnaire

Structured questions covering what data you collect, how consent is captured, retention periods, third parties you share data with, and breach readiness - the parts a scan can't see.

PHASE 03

Manual Review

I go through the scan results and your questionnaire answers together, flag the gaps, and prioritize them by actual regulatory and reputational risk - not a generic checklist.

How it runs

You submit your domain and complete the questionnaire → I review both → You get a prioritized report

No automated compliance score. No generic checklist. You get a report written by a person who actually read your answers.
