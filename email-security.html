What it covers

Three phases: a DNS authentication scan, a spoofing risk assessment, and a manual review of both.

PHASE 01

DNS Authentication Scan

Checks your domain's SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records for presence, correct syntax, and policy strength - the three records that decide whether email claiming to be from you gets trusted or blocked.

PHASE 02

Spoofing Risk Assessment

Flags whether your domain can currently be spoofed and how easily - a missing or misconfigured record is often the only thing standing between your name and someone else's phishing email.

PHASE 03

Manual Review

I go through the scan results myself and write up the exact DNS records to add, in priority order - not a generic "improve your email security" summary.
