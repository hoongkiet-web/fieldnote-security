The written record confirming you're authorized to have Fieldnote Security scan the domain(s) below. This is a quick step, not a hurdle - it happens after we've already talked, not instead of getting in touch.

Why this exists: running a security scan against a domain without permission can fall foul of the Computer Misuse Act 1990, even when the intent is defensive. This form is simply the paper trail that confirms you've given that permission - it protects you as much as it protects the work. All fields are required.
