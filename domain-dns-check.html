What it covers

Three phases: a DNS health scan, an attack surface snapshot, and a manual review of both.

PHASE 01

DNS Health Scan

Checks MX, A, NS, and TXT records for misconfigurations, dangling entries, and subdomain takeover risk.

PHASE 02

Attack Surface Snapshot

Identifies which ports and services are reachable from the internet, and what's exposed that shouldn't be.

PHASE 03

Manual Review

Personal review of both, prioritized by what's misconfigured versus what's genuinely exposed.

How it runs

You submit your domain → I run the scan and review it → You get a prioritized report of what's exposed and misconfigured
